Installation

From survey to switch-on in four steps.

STEP 1

Site survey

An engineer visits to check your roof or ground space, your power board and your hours of power cuts.

STEP 2

System design

We size the panels, battery and inverter to your appliances and the way you use power.

STEP 3

Installation

Eversol technicians install and configure the system, including the Wi-Fi connection.

STEP 4

Monitoring

Follow your system on your phone over Wi-Fi. After-sales service and warranty are handled from our showroom in Erbil.
